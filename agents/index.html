Book Jon with an AI assistant

Planning an event with ChatGPT, Claude or another AI assistant? It can check Jon's dates, give you an exact price and hold your date — no forms, no waiting for a reply.

How it works

  1. Your assistant looks up Jon's shows, prices and open dates.
  2. It gets an exact quote for your date, start time and show.
  3. Once you approve, it holds the date and you get a link to sign the agreement and pay the deposit.
  4. That's it — your booking is confirmed the moment the deposit is paid.
