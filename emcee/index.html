Do you offer nonprofit pricing?

How far in advance should I book?

As early as you can. Popular dates fill up, and bookings need at least 14 days' notice.

Do you travel outside Indianapolis?

Yes. Jon is based in Indianapolis and performs across Indiana and beyond. Travel outside the Indianapolis area may be quoted separately.

How do I lock in my date?

Request your date through the booking form, by email or by phone. Once Jon confirms, you'll get an agreement to sign, and a 50% deposit secures the date.

Can I book with an AI assistant?

Yes. Assistants like ChatGPT and Claude can check Jon's dates, get an exact quote and hold your date. See jonmobley.com/agents.

Request your date and Jon will get back to you, or email booking@jonmobley.com or call 317-426-1270.
