About Jon Mobley

Jon Mobley is a magician, comedian and emcee based in Indianapolis. He entertains crowds with his magnetic personality, quick wit and jaw-dropping surprises.

Quick facts

  • Based in Indianapolis, Indiana. Performs across Indiana and beyond.
  • Penn & Teller: Fool Us on The CW, twice: a bullet catch in Season 9 and “Literal Magic” in Season 11.
  • Featured performer at the Chicago Magic Lounge.
  • On TV in Indy: regular magic segments on Fox 59 IndyNow and WISH-TV.
  • Shows: Sleight of Mind, his comedy stage show; strolling close-up magic; and emceeing for galas, fundraisers and corporate events.
  • Rated 5.0 from 139 Google reviews.
